About Me

Hi, I’m Linda. I believe therapy works best when it feels like a genuine human conversation.

Welcome! Whether you are navigating anxiety, burnout, grief, or a major life transition, my goal is to offer a calm, grounded, and collaborative space where you feel genuinely heard.

Rather than just nodding along, I combine deep emotional insight with practical, real-world tools you can use between sessions to build boundaries and peace of mind.
